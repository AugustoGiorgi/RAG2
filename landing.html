The return workflow

Three stations. One return, start to finish.

The core of RAG Tax AI mirrors how an engagement already moves through your firm — and the AI carries the load at each station.

01

Preparation

financials → workpaper

Drop in the P&L, balance sheet, prior workpapers, W-2s and 1099s — phone-scanned PDFs included; the AI reads them visually.

  • Book-to-tax reconciliation built on fixed, deterministic treatment rules
  • Every total a live formula — edit one number, the whole workbook reflows
  • Data-entry guide for your tax software, field by field, with live tie-outs
02

Review

return → second opinion

Upload the finished return with its support and get the read a senior reviewer would give it before signature.

  • Return lines tied out against the source financials
  • Findings ranked by risk, each with the authority behind it
  • A filing-readiness verdict and the open-question list
03

Deliverable

work → client-ready

Turn the reviewed engagement into something a client can actually read and act on.

  • Plain-language summaries of what was filed and why
  • Consistent, firm-branded document output
  • Ready to send — once your professional signs off
