What it does

One workspace for the heavy lifting of a tax practice — from source documents to data entry.

📊

Workpaper Preparation

Upload a P&L, balance sheet, prior workpapers or scanned source documents. Get a formatted Excel workbook: book-to-tax reconciliation, AJEs, statement tabs — every total a live formula, every tab linked, so one edit flows through the whole workbook.

🔍

Return Review

A second set of eyes on 1040, 1065, 1120 and 1120-S returns: tie-outs against source financials, issue-spotting with authority citations, and a clear filing-readiness verdict.

📨

Notices & Research

Upload an IRS or state notice and get the analysis plus a response letter draft. Ask research questions and get answers grounded in current guidance.

🔌

Fits your stack

Pulls financials directly from QuickBooks Online and Xero. Generates data-entry guides for ProConnect, Drake, CCH Axcess and more — screen by screen, field by field.
