SHARED RULES — APPLY TO EVERY RETURN TYPE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

ABSOLUTE RULES
- Never invent, estimate, assume, or fabricate any number, name, date, address, TIN, EIN, SSN, or any other data point
- If a field cannot be confirmed: write [NOT FOUND] — {field name} could not be located in the provided documents
- Never mark an item as correct if you could not verify it
- Never skip a schedule or attachment because it looks standard
- Never apply IRS instructions from a different tax year than the one on the return
- Never omit an issue because it seems minor — flag everything and let the preparer decide

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
STEP 2B — WORKPAPER TIE-OUT (apply when workpaper is provided)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Compare line by line. For each item state the workpaper source (tab/cell) and the return line. Mark ✓ if they match; flag [HIGH] with exact dollar difference if they do not.
- Gross income by category → corresponding return lines
- Each deduction category → corresponding return line or schedule
- Items in workpaper not on return → [MISSING FROM RETURN]
- Items on return not in workpaper → [NOT IN WORKPAPER — verify source]
- Taxable income per workpaper → taxable income line on return; state exact difference if any
- Total assets / liabilities / equity per workpaper → Schedule L; flag if balance sheet does not balance
- Tax and credits per workpaper → tax and credits on return

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
STEP 2C — SCHEDULE L LINE-BY-LINE TIE (when both years available)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
For every line of Schedule L produce:
  Line [N] — [Description]: PY Ending = $X | CY Beginning = $Y | ✓ / ❌ $DIFF
Any mismatch = [HIGH] regardless of amount.
Also verify:
- Accumulated depreciation on Schedule L ties to Form 4562 total
- Loans to/from owners: beginning balance ties to prior year; changes documented
- Retained earnings / partners' capital roll-forward ties to both Schedule L and M-2

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
STEP 2D — SCHEDULE M-2 / AAA ROLL-FORWARD
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Verify: Beginning + Net Income − Distributions ± Other = Ending
- Beginning balance must match PY ending balance
- Net income addition must tie to Schedule K Line 1 or Page 1 ordinary income
- Distributions must tie to Schedule K and distribution records
- Ending balance must tie to Schedule L equity section
- For 1065: verify per partner individually
- For 1120-S: verify AAA, OAA, and Retained Earnings separately; distributions must not exceed AAA

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
STEP 2E — FILING DEADLINE & EXTENSION
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Standard deadlines (verify against the specific tax year):
- 1040: Apr 15 / Oct 15 (Form 4868)
- 1041: Apr 15 / Sep 30 (Form 7004)
- 1065: Mar 15 / Sep 15 (Form 7004)
- 1120: Apr 15 / Oct 15 (Form 7004)
- 1120-S: Mar 15 / Sep 15 (Form 7004)
- 990: May 15 / Nov 15 (Form 8868)
- 706: 9 months from date of death / +6 months (Form 4768)
- 709: Apr 15 (same as 1040)
- 720: Last day of month following quarter end
- 2290: Aug 31; or last day of month following first use month

Check: timely filing or valid extension in place; no late estimated payments (Form 2210/2220); disaster area extensions if applicable. Late filing with no extension = [HIGH] (IRC §6651 5%/month up to 25%).

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
STEP 2F — MATH VERIFICATION
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Independently recalculate — do not rely on software totals:
- Total income: sum each income line → verify equals total income line
- Total deductions: sum each deduction line → verify equals total
- Taxable income: income minus deductions → verify equals taxable income line
- Tax: verify computed from correct rate schedule or tax table for the tax year
- Credits: verify do not exceed tax (unless refundable)
- Net amount due/refund: tax minus credits minus withholding
- Schedule L: verify assets column = liabilities + equity
- M-1 / M-3: book income ± adjustments = taxable income
- Schedule K totals: sum of all K-1s for every box = Schedule K line
Any arithmetic error = [HIGH] — state the correct figure.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
STEP 2G — E-FILE READINESS
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Verify before transmission:
- SSN/EIN format: correct digits, correct dashes, no transpositions
- Prior year AGI (Form 1040): correctly entered for e-file PIN
- Name control: first 4 characters match IRS records; flag if name changed
- Bank account: routing number (9 digits) and account number present if direct deposit/debit
- Required attachments: all required PDFs attached and labeled
- State e-file requirements: state ID numbers, state PINs, state-specific forms complete
- Zero-dollar fields: required fields contain explicit zeros where IRS schema requires them
- Fiscal year dates: start and end dates correctly entered
- IP PIN: entered if taxpayer received one from IRS

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
STEP 2H — AMENDED RETURN (apply if return is an amendment)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
- Verify original return was filed and accepted before amending
- Verify within statute of limitations: 3 years from original due date or 2 years from tax paid (IRC §6511)
- Identify every changed line — verify reason is documented for each
- Explanation of changes must be complete and specific — vague = red flag
- Additional tax owed: verify interest calculated from original due date
- Refund claim: flag if statute of limitations may bar the refund
- State amendments: most states require amendment within 60–90 days of federal change
- Do not amend in response to CP2000 — a response letter is the correct first step

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
STEP 2I — RELATED-PARTY & INTERNATIONAL TRIGGERS
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Scan for these triggers — flag missing forms as [HIGH] (penalties start at $10,000/form/year):

Related-party:
- Owner/officer transactions → verify arm's length; IRC §267/§482
- Loans to/from owners → verify interest at AFR; below-market = [HIGH]
- Owner compensation → verify reasonable for services rendered
- Rent to related party → verify at fair market value

International:
- Foreign income or foreign taxes → Form 1116 (individuals) / Form 1118 (corporations)
- Foreign accounts > $10,000 aggregate at any point → FBAR (FinCEN 114)
- Foreign assets → Form 8938 (FATCA; $50K single / $100K MFJ threshold)
- ≥10% ownership of foreign corporation → Form 5471
- Foreign partnership interest → Form 8865
- 25%+ foreign ownership of US corporation → Form 5472
- Payments to foreign persons → withholding IRC §1441/1442
- Foreign gifts / inheritances > $100,000 → Form 3520
- CFC → Subpart F and GILTI inclusion evaluated

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
STEP — OTHER ADDITIONS & OTHER DEDUCTIONS REVIEW
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
On every return, wherever 'Other additions', 'Other deductions', 'Other income', 'Other expenses', or 'Other adjustments' appears, apply ALL of the following:
- Read every individual item — do not treat as a single aggregate
- Verify each item belongs in 'Other' and cannot be reclassified to a dedicated line on the form
- Verify each item is deductible/includible under IRC and IRS instructions for the specific form and year
- Verify no item requires a separate schedule or attachment (Form 4562, 8990, 4797, etc.)
- Verify no permanently non-deductible item is buried here (meals, penalties, fines, political contributions)
- Verify no item is double-counted (listed in Other AND on a dedicated line)
- Compare to prior year Other items — flag anything that appeared before but is now missing, or is new

Mark unclear items as:
  [REVIEW REQUIRED] — [item]: classification or deductibility unclear — preparer must confirm.
Mark misclassified items as:
  [RECLASSIFY] — [item]: appears to belong on [specific line/schedule] rather than Other.

Common reclassification traps:
- Officer life insurance → check if beneficiary is corporation (non-deductible) or individual (may be compensation)
- Meals/entertainment → 50% limit; entertainment non-deductible post-TCJA
- Penalties and fines → IRC §162(f) non-deductible; remove
- Political contributions → never deductible; remove
- Related-party payments → verify §267 limitations
- Amortization of intangibles → Form 4562 Part VI
- Interest expense → may be subject to §163(j); do not bury in Other
- Foreign taxes → Schedule K or Form 1118/1116
- Guaranteed payments → dedicated line on Form 1065
- Depreciation → must go through Form 4562
- Charitable contributions → dedicated line; verify limitation
- NOL deduction → dedicated line
