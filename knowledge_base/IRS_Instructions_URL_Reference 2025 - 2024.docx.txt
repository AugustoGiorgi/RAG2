HOW TO USE THIS DOCUMENT

Upload this file alongside the tax return being reviewed. The agent will read the URLs and access the relevant IRS instructions directly to verify each line item, threshold, limitation, and required attachment for the specific tax year.



Instructions for the agent:

Identify the return type and tax year from the return being reviewed.

Fetch the corresponding TY 2025 or TY 2024 instructions URL from the table below.

Apply the instructions for that exact tax year — never use a different year's instructions.

For any line where treatment appears incorrect, cite the specific instruction section by name and page.

Check the 'Post-Release Changes' URL at the bottom of the table for any late corrections to the instructions.

QUICK REFERENCE — KEY THRESHOLDS & DEADLINES (TY 2025)

Verify these against the fetched instructions — amounts may change annually.



Filing Deadlines

Form 1040: April 15, 2026 / Extended: October 15, 2026 (Form 4868)

Form 1041: April 15, 2026 / Extended: September 30, 2026 (Form 7004)

Form 1065: March 15, 2026 / Extended: September 15, 2026 (Form 7004)

Form 1120: April 15, 2026 / Extended: October 15, 2026 (Form 7004)

Form 1120-S: March 15, 2026 / Extended: September 15, 2026 (Form 7004)

Form 990: May 15, 2026 / Extended: November 15, 2026 (Form 8868)

Form 706: 9 months from date of death / +6 months (Form 4768)

Form 720: Last day of month following end of each quarter

Form 2290: August 31, 2025 (July 1 vehicles); last day of month following first use month



Key 2025 Thresholds (verify against fetched instructions)

Standard deduction — Single: $15,000 | MFJ: $30,000 | MFS: $15,000 | HOH: $22,500

Standard deduction — 65+/blind add-on: $2,000 (single) | $1,600 (MFJ each)

SALT deduction cap: $10,000 (unchanged)

QBI deduction phase-in — MFJ: $394,600–$494,600 | Others: $197,300–$247,300

Gift tax annual exclusion: $19,000 per donee

Estate/gift tax lifetime exclusion: $13,990,000

Social Security wage base (SE tax): $176,100

Section 179 expensing limit: $1,250,000 | Phase-out begins: $3,130,000

Bonus depreciation: 40% (2025 phase-down)

NOL deduction: 80% of taxable income (post-2017 NOLs)

FBAR threshold: $10,000 aggregate balance at any point during year

FATCA Form 8938: $50,000 (single year-end) / $75,000 (year high) — Single; double for MFJ

Form 5472 — 25%+ foreign-owned US corporation: $10,000/year penalty if missing

Form 5471 — 10%+ ownership foreign corp: $10,000/year penalty if missing



Schedule L / M-2 Triggers

Schedule M-3 required (instead of M-1): total assets ≥ $10M at year-end

Schedule B-1 (Form 1065): any partner owns 50%+ of profits or capital

Schedule K-2 / K-3: required if any foreign activity, foreign partners, or foreign income — check instructions for domestic filing exceptions
